# Fixing Your Feed

**Live site:** https://deantrenkamp.github.io/Fixing-Your-Feed/

Created by **Dean Trenkamp** for Multimedia Artifact Project in INFO-I 310, Indiana University, Fall 2026.

## About

Fixing Your Feed is a website for 18- to 24-year-olds who spend hours a day on social media. It explains in plain language how recommendation algorithms decide what shows up in your feed, then helps you reset your feed on purpose.

## Pages

- **How Algorithms Work:** how feeds get built, what TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube track, and what researchers have found.
- **Reset Guide:** step-by-step checklists for TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube, with the evidence behind each step. Progress saves in your browser.
- **Quiz:** six questions that rank which of your habits shape your feed the most, with research-backed fixes.

## Built with
