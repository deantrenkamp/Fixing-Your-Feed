Before you start

Do the steps in order. The first one or two do the most; the rest keep your feed from drifting back.

Menu names can change when apps update. If you can't find a setting, search for it in the app's settings.

    Keep it clean

    A reset fades if your habits don't change. These three matter on every app.

    Swipe past fastPausing counts as interest. The WSJ's bots trained TikTok just by lingering.
    Block sources, not postsBlocking a channel stopped 43% of unwanted YouTube picks; flagging one video stopped 11% (Mozilla).
    Check in monthlyFeeds keep learning. Revisit your topics, follows, and history once a month.
