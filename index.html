Fixing Your Feed

The big question

How do social media algorithms decide what you see?

Your feed isn't in time order. Each app ranks posts by what it predicts you'll watch, like, or share, and it learns from every swipe. Here's how that works, platform by platform, and what the research says.

How a feed gets built

Old wayChronologicalNewest posts first, only from accounts you follow.
NowRankedPosts ordered by predicted interest, from anyone on the app.
  1. 1CollectThe app logs what you watch, like, share, search, and skip, plus how long you pause.
  2. 2FilterIt narrows millions of posts to a pool you might care about.
  3. 3RankEach post gets a score for how likely you are to engage with it.
  4. 4LearnYour reaction feeds back in, and the next feed adjusts.

Most apps mix all three, and machine learning keeps tuning them as you scroll.

What researchers found

Wall Street Journal · 2021

Pausing is a signal, even if you never tap like

Reporters ran 100 bot accounts on TikTok. The bots never searched or liked; they only lingered on or rewatched certain videos. TikTok figured out their interests in under two hours.

93%of videos shown to one bot that lingered on sad videos were about sadness and depression.
Mozilla Foundation · 2022

A study of 22,722 YouTube volunteers measured how many unwanted recommendations each control prevented.

U.S. Surgeon General · 2023

Time on the feed adds up

The Surgeon General's advisory found that adolescents who spend more than 3 hours a day on social media face double the risk of depression and anxiety symptoms.

2×the risk at 3+ hours a day. The finding is about teens, but it's a reason anyone should notice where their scrolling time goes.

FAQ

Can I reset my feed completely?

Close to it. TikTok's Refresh your For You feed and Instagram's Reset suggested content both start your recommendations over. On YouTube, clearing watch history does the most. What you do afterward rebuilds the feed, so habits matter.

Does chronological order still exist?

Sometimes. Instagram lets you switch Feed to only Following or Favorites, shown newest first.

What counts more, likes or watch time?

Watch time is a big one. TikTok says finishing a longer video is a strong interest signal, and the WSJ's bots shaped their feeds without liking anything.

Does tapping "Not interested" actually work?

A little. Mozilla found it stopped only 11% of unwanted YouTube recommendations, while Don't recommend channel stopped 43%. Blocking a source beats flagging single posts.
